Introduction

Online shopping has become a popular choice because of convenience, variety and competitive prices. This project studies shopping behaviour and provides product links collected for the project.

Objectives

  • Study online shopping behaviour.
  • Identify preferred product categories.
  • Analyse shopping frequency and payment methods.
  • Provide direct product links across shopping platforms.
